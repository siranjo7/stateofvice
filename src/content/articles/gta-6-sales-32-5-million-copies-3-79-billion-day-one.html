Confirmed: Rockstar Games says Grand Theft Auto VI sold 32.5 million copies and made $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales. Outlets report the figures as a Rockstar statement, issued on Nov 20.

GTA 6 launched on Nov 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A day later, Rockstar put numbers on it. The company calls it the biggest entertainment launch ever. Here is what the figures say, and what they do not.

What Rockstar reported

  • $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales, worldwide. Coverage says this runs through the end of Nov 19.
  • 32.5 million copies sold.
  • A quote from Sam Houser: "It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto 6," and "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world."

PC Gamer and GamingBolt report the day-one figure. Push Square reports the copy count. For comparison, GamingBolt gives about $800 million for the GTA V launch record, a figure from its own coverage.

What the numbers do not tell you

Read with care: Outlets disagree on what the 32.5 million covers. Push Square says it is copies sold during the preload period. Shattered says units sold "before release." We could not read Rockstar's own wording. Some headlines called the figure pre-orders. That is not a safe reading.
  • Retail sales are not revenue. The $3.79 billion is described as a company estimate of consumer spending. Shattered notes that Reuters and the AP did not call it Take-Two revenue. Take-Two has not published revenue for this launch.
  • No breakdown. Coverage reports no split by platform, edition, region or digital versus physical.
  • The records are Rockstar's claim. "Biggest entertainment launch" is the company's framing, not an independent ranking.

Estimates are not results

Before launch, analyst firms such as Newzoo and Sensor Tower published projections. Those were estimates. Only the Rockstar figures above are company-reported. Any platform split you read, such as a claim that most sales are on PS5, is unconfirmed.

What comes next

Take-Two said earlier that it would share more about GTA 6 performance after launch. Its last official outlook was up to $8.2 billion in FY2027 Net Bookings. See our earnings story. For critic scores, see our reviews story.

Quick FAQ

How many copies did GTA 6 sell?

Rockstar reports 32.5 million copies. Outlets describe the window differently: Push Square says the preload period, Shattered says before release. We could not read Rockstar's wording ourselves.

Is $3.79 billion Take-Two's revenue?

No. Coverage describes it as day-one retail sales, a company estimate of consumer spending. Take-Two has not published revenue for the launch.

Has Take-Two given GTA 6 earnings guidance for this launch?

Not in this announcement. Its last official outlook was up to $8.2 billion in FY2027 Net Bookings, stated on Nov 9.
