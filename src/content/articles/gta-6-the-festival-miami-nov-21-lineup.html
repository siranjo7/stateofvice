Confirmed: Rockstar and PlayStation will hold Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival on Saturday, November 21, 2026 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, two days after launch. Travis Scott, Cardi B, Metro Boomin and Justice are on the bill.

Rockstar announced the launch party on Nov 9. It's a live show with artists from the game and its soundtrack, across two stages. We read the announcement on Rockstar's Newswire.

The lineup

  • Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Sofaygo
  • Cardi B
  • Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso
  • Justice (DJ set)
  • Metro Boomin
  • Nick León B2B DJ Python
  • Prospa B2B Skream
  • Rauw & Friends
  • Suero
  • Tainy
  • The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set)

The event is presented with PlayStation and produced by the team behind Miami's III Points festival.

How tickets work

Tickets go through a pre-sale lottery on DICE, not a normal sale. The lottery closed at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10. Winners can buy tickets from Wednesday, November 11, with a limit of two per lottery code. Rockstar points fans to gtavi-thefestival.miami for details.

Not confirmed: set times, ticket prices, and whether any tickets will go on general sale. Rockstar has not announced a livestream.

Why it matters

Music is a big part of this launch. Travis Scott and Metro Boomin both appear on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, and Justice made the original score. See our radio stations guide for what plays in the game.
