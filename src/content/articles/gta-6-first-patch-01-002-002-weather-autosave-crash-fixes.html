Confirmed: Rockstar released its first post-launch GTA 6 patch, version 01.002.002, around November 23, 2026. The notes list fixes for crashes, weather, autosaving and more on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Four days after launch, Rockstar fixed several bugs players had noticed. The headline item is weather that did not change for long stretches. The patch is about 603 MB, per VGC.

What the patch notes say (CONFIRMED)

VGC quotes Rockstar's notes. The list reads, in short:

  • Fixed several issues that caused the game to crash.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods.
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly.
  • Fixed several issues that resulted in phone calls not triggering correctly. This includes calls that progress the story or unlock missions.
  • Fixed an issue that left players unable to lose their Wanted Level.
  • Fixed an issue that left players unable to use the deadlift bar in the Gym.
  • Fixed partners not handing over bags, so players could not sell them to fences.

About the weather

Some players said GTA 6 had no rain. Rockstar's note says weather was not changing for extended periods. That points to a bug in how often conditions changed. It does not say weather was missing.

Not confirmed: Kotaku argues that storms do exist in the game and that some players may simply be outside the area where it rains. Rockstar has not said this. Treat it as one outlet's view. For what Rockstar has shown of storms before launch, see the Game Informer cover story.

What the patch does not say

Rockstar did not add a statement beyond the notes, per VGC. The list does not mention performance modes, new content or a PC version. GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

Earlier pre-launch updates were 01.002.000 and 01.002.001. Rockstar did not publish notes for those.

Quick FAQ

What is GTA 6 patch 01.002.002?

It is the first post-launch update for GTA 6, released around November 23, 2026. Rockstar's notes list fixes for crashes, weather, autosaving, phone calls, Wanted Level, a gym bug and a bag-selling bug.

Did Rockstar add rain or new weather?

No. The notes say Rockstar fixed an issue where weather did not change for extended periods. They do not describe new weather types.

Is there a PC patch?

No. GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no official PC version.
