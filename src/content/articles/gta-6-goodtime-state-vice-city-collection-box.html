Confirmed: On September 24, 2026, Rockstar opened pre-orders for The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection, a limited-edition collector's box priced at $399.99 in the US (£349.99 in the UK). It arrives November 19, 2026, while supplies last. It does not include the game.

This is the closest thing GTA 6 has to a "collector's edition," but it's merchandise only. If you want the game, you still need to buy the Standard ($79.99) or Ultimate ($99.99) edition separately.

What's in the box

  • 6" figure of Macca the Gator, with a stash compartment
  • Matte black GTA VI Oakley Frogskins sunglasses with Prizm Sapphire lenses
  • New Era 9FORTY A-Frame snapback with Vice City / Leonida embellishments
  • Macca the Gator magnetic mirror
  • Vice City swizzle spoon
  • Vice City razor blade keychain
  • Leonida Keys crossbody bag
  • Chunkee the Manatee shot glass
  • Leonida Keys enamel pin set in a metal tin
  • Premium stickers in a stash bag
  • Double-sided souvenir poster with a map of Leonida

Is it worth it?

That's up to you. It's a fan collectible, not a game edition. There are no in-game items listed, and nothing in the box affects the game. If you're shipping it abroad, note that some outlets have pointed out the razor blade keychain could cause trouble with airport security or customs.

Still unconfirmed: a collector's game edition. Rockstar's only game editions are Standard and Ultimate. See our editions guide.
