Confirmed: On October 29, 2026, Rockstar Games and the French duo Justice announced Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score. It releases December 11, 2026. A new track, "Pusher" featuring Gesaffelstein, is out now.

Rockstar had already said Justice wrote GTA 6's score. Now the score has a release date. It comes three weeks after the game's November 19 launch.

What was announced

  • Release date: December 11, 2026. Pre-orders are open.
  • Labels: Because Music, Ed Banger and Rockstar Games.
  • Formats: streaming, CD and vinyl. Reports describe a 2xCD and 3xLP set, in a standard black and a limited clear edition.
  • Length: 30 main tracks, plus bonus digital tracks. Together they make a suite of about three hours, per WorthPlaying.
  • How it plays: Rockstar says the score responds to how players move through Vice City and Leonida.

"Pusher" with Gesaffelstein

"Pusher" is the second preview from the score. Press describes it as a long, brooding instrumental. It is the first time Justice has worked with fellow French producer Gesaffelstein. The first preview was "Flowers in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, released October 15.

Track count differs by outlet: most outlets report 30 tracks. Stereogum and EDMTunes cite 50, which appears to include the bonus digital tracks. We use 30 main tracks until Rockstar publishes a full tracklist.

Not the same as The Album

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is a different release. It is 34 original songs by many artists, out November 19 with the game. The score is Justice's instrumental music for missions and the open world. The in-game radio stations are a third thing.

No. The Album is 34 songs out November 19. The Original Score is Justice's separate instrumental music.
