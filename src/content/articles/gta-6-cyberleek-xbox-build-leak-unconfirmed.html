Rumor: a leaker group called Cyberleek says it has posted a development build of GTA 6 for Xbox. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed this. We could not verify the files, and we are not linking to them.

Cyberleek says it released an "Xbox Build 1.0.2.1 (Nov 2026)" days after launch. Reports say the main executable is missing, so nobody can run it yet. Treat all of this as unverified.

What is claimed (RUMOR)

  • The build: VGC reported on November 19 that the group says it holds the full November 2026 build and is sharing it by torrent. Every file except the main executable is said to be available.
  • The countdown: per VGC, the executable is "held on a countdown." TweakTown (November 22) reports the group says it will release it on Monday, November 23, or sooner if its crypto token hits a $15 million market cap. VGC's earlier wording was a "certain market value."
  • PC talk: Cyberleek says the community could have the game running on PC within a week. TweakTown notes nobody has independently shown the files running as a full game.
  • Other views: TweakTown says VX Underground reviewed the files, found game assets and believes the leak is genuine. That is one outside opinion, not confirmation.

What Rockstar and Take-Two have said

Neither company has responded to this specific claim, according to VGC. VGC does note that Take-Two has called its investigation into the leaker "rapidly evolving" and has filed subpoenas to Discord. TweakTown reports Rockstar has warned it will treat any illegal PC port "very seriously."

Be careful: coverage warns that downloads claiming to be this build have been linked to malware. The group is also tied to a crypto token. Don't download anything.

What is confirmed

Confirmed: GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no official PC version. See the separate PC report (also a rumor).

Quick FAQ

Has GTA 6 leaked?

Cyberleek claims to have posted an Xbox development build. The claim is unconfirmed. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed it, and the main executable is reportedly missing, so the build can't be run.

Is a GTA 6 PC version coming from this leak?

No official PC version exists. Cyberleek says the community could get the game running on PC within a week. No one has independently shown that working.

Should I download the leaked files?

No. Coverage warns that downloads claiming to be the build have been linked to malware, and Rockstar has warned about legal action over illegal ports.
