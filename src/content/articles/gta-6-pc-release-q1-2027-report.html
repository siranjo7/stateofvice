Rumor: on October 13, 2026, GamesBeat reported, citing four industry sources familiar with Rockstar's plans, that the PC version of GTA 6 is planned for January–March 2027. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed a PC version or a date.
What's confirmed: GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There's no PC version at launch and no official PC system requirements.

Why this report is worth taking seriously

  • Multiple sources: the report cites four people, not one anonymous leaker.
  • The business logic fits: January–March 2027 is Take-Two's fiscal Q4, so a PC launch there would count toward the same fiscal year as the console launch. CEO Strauss Zelnick has talked more and more about PC's importance.

Why we're still calling it a rumor

It hasn't been officially announced, and Rockstar's history points the other way. GTA 5 reached PC about 18 months after consoles. Red Dead Redemption 2 took about 13 months. A gap of roughly four months would be a big change. That's possible, but we won't mark it confirmed until Rockstar does.

If you're a PC player

Don't buy upgrades for "official GTA 6 PC specs." None exist yet, and any spec sheet you see is made up. If you want to play in November, you'll need a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. See whether you need a PS5 Pro (you don't).
