Confirmed: Rockstar says Grand Theft Auto VI targets 30 frames per second on current consoles. The PS5 Pro adds sharper image quality through PSSR and ray-traced shadows found only on the Pro. The game will also stream through Xbox Cloud Gaming, including on Xbox One.

Rockstar's Nov 10 pre-load post answered the biggest open performance questions nine days before the November 19 launch. We read the post on Rockstar's Newswire.

30 FPS on every console

Rockstar says the game was built "from the ground up" for current consoles and is "targeting 30 frames per second." It says this keeps the density and detail of Leonida's places and people. The post names no 60 FPS or performance mode.

Rockstar does not list a higher frame rate for the PS5 Pro. What the Pro gets is a cleaner image, not more frames.

What the PS5 Pro adds

  • PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution): Sony's AI upscaling, which sharpens the picture.
  • Ray-traced shadows, which Rockstar says are only available on PS5 Pro.

Every PS5 also gets DualSense haptic feedback across the game (adjustable in accessibility settings), Tempest 3D Audio and faster loading.

Xbox One can play it, through the cloud

Rockstar says GTA 6 will be available through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. Rockstar notes that connection speed can affect how it runs. It is a stream, not a download to the console. The game also supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox.

Not confirmed: a 40 FPS or 60 FPS mode. Some outlets report pre-load data hints at a newer PSSR version and 120Hz support. That is datamining, not a Rockstar statement. Rockstar also has not given resolutions for any console.

Quick FAQ

What frame rate does GTA 6 run at?

Is GTA 6 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

Rockstar does not list a higher frame rate for the PS5 Pro. The Pro gets sharper visuals through PSSR and ray-traced shadows that only the Pro has.
