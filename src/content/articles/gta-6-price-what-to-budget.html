You saw the headlines before launch — "GTA 6 is $80," "the collector's edition is $230." Here's the honest, updated version now that Rockstar has actually confirmed the numbers: the standard edition is $79.99, the Ultimate Edition is $99.99, and that once-unconfirmed "$80 leak" turned out to be almost exactly right.

What's confirmed: GTA 6 standard edition is $79.99. The Ultimate Edition is $99.99. Both are confirmed by Rockstar Games as of June 25, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Pre-orders are open now.

The signal that got closest (and it wasn't a leaker)

Before the official reveal, the most reliable pointer came from the company itself: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick indicated GTA 6 would likely land in the $70–$80 range, in line with current AAA pricing. The official number landed right at the top of that range.

The "$80 leak" — it turned out to be right

Update: a retail listing leaked pre-launch showing an $80 standard edition. At the time, that listing used placeholder codes and filler prices, so it genuinely wasn't a confirmed price yet — treating it as unverified was the correct call. But now that Rockstar has published the real number, the leak turns out to have been essentially accurate: $79.99 is a rounding error from the $80 everyone was passing around. Being skeptical of an unconfirmed listing and it turning out to be roughly true aren't in conflict — that's exactly how "rumor until confirmed" is supposed to work.

What to actually budget

  • If you already own a PS5 or Xbox Series console: budget $79.99 for the standard edition. You don't need a new console — and despite the rumors, you do not need a PS5 Pro to play it.
  • If you want the Ultimate Edition contents: budget $99.99. Worth it if you want the exclusive vehicles, shops and outfits from day one — otherwise the standard edition gets you the same story.
  • If you want the online-mode bonuses: wait to see what GTA Online 2 bonuses actually include before paying extra for a tier based on that alone.
  • Everyone pre-ordering: the Vintage Vice City Pack (free with digital purchases before Nov 20, 2026) and a free month of GTA+ come with digital pre-orders at no extra cost.

For most people, the honest answer is: $79.99, standard edition, add the Ultimate Edition only if its specific extras are worth $20 more to you.
