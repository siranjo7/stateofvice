Confirmed: In its Nov 9, 2026 earnings release, Take-Two lists Grand Theft Auto VI for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. CEO Strauss Zelnick says the launch "is ten days away."

Take-Two reported fiscal second-quarter results on Nov 9. The release keeps the launch date, says pre-orders keep reaching "unprecedented levels," and holds the full-year outlook. We read the release in its SEC filing.

What the release says

The release lists Grand Theft Auto VI for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. Zelnick says: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away." He also says pre-orders are "continuing to reach unprecedented levels." The release gives no pre-order number.

The numbers

Net Bookings for the quarter were $1.60 billion. That is down 18% from $1.96 billion a year earlier. Take-Two kept its fiscal 2027 outlook for Net Bookings of $8.0 to $8.2 billion. The company reiterated it. It did not raise it.

Some outlets, including Variety, say Zelnick told analysts presales are "far in excess" of expectations. Some reports also quote him saying Rockstar has completed development. That comes from the call, not the written release, and we have not read a full transcript. Treat the wording as reported, not as our own confirmation.

Not confirmed: pre-order totals. Third-party estimates, such as about 7.7 million digital pre-orders from Sensor Tower as cited by Reuters, are analyst data. Take-Two has not published a figure. Reports also differ on whether the forecast moved. Take-Two's own release says it did not.

What it means for launch

Nothing about the date or platforms changed. Pre-load opens today on PS5, per our pre-load story. There is still no PC version at launch. See the PC report for the unconfirmed Q1 2027 claim. For the earlier date statement, see Take-Two's August reaffirmation.
