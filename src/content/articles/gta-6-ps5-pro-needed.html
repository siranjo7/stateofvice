Short answer: no. You do not need a PlayStation 5 Pro to play GTA 6. A standard PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S runs it. On Nov 10 Rockstar finally gave official performance details, so here is what the Pro does and does not add.

Confirmed (Rockstar, Nov 10): GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on current consoles. The PS5 Pro adds sharper visuals through PSSR (Sony's AI upscaling) and ray-traced shadows that only the Pro has. Rockstar lists no higher frame rate for the Pro. Full details: our performance story.

The old rumor, now outdated

Rumor, now contradicted: leakers claimed GTA 6 would hit 60 FPS only on the PS5 Pro. Rockstar's official post says the game targets 30 FPS on consoles and lists image quality and shadows, not frame rate, as the Pro's extras. A 40 FPS or 60 FPS mode is still unconfirmed; some outlets cite pre-load data, but Rockstar has said nothing about it.

So do you actually need one?

In plain terms:

  • To play GTA 6: you need a PS5, or an Xbox Series X or S. If you own any of those, you're set.
  • A PS5 Pro is optional. It gives a sharper picture and better shadows, not a smoother frame rate. Worth it only if image quality matters a lot to you.
  • For most people, a base PS5 will look and play great. Rockstar games are famous for looking excellent even on base hardware.

Don't buy a $700 console for frame rate

Don't spend about $700 on a PS5 Pro because a thumbnail said GTA 6 is "unplayable" without one. Rockstar's own details say every console targets 30 FPS. The Pro's extras are a sharper picture (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows. If you already have a PS5 or Series X|S, do nothing. If you're buying a console now, get the Pro only if the best-looking version matters to you.

Quick Xbox note

Same logic on Xbox. GTA 6 runs on both the Series X and Series S, and Rockstar lists no Xbox-specific extras. Rockstar also confirms Xbox Cloud Gaming, so an Xbox One can stream the game using Series S graphics settings; connection speed affects how well it runs.

Quick FAQ

Can you play GTA 6 on a normal PS5?

Yes. GTA 6 is confirmed for the standard PlayStation 5. The PS5 Pro is an optional upgrade, not a requirement.

Will GTA 6 be 60 FPS on PS5 or PS5 Pro?

No 60 FPS mode is confirmed. Rockstar says GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on consoles. The PS5 Pro adds PSSR and ray-traced shadows, not a higher frame rate.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No PC version has been officially announced. At launch it's PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. A GamesBeat report says PC is coming in January–March 2027, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. See the PC report.

How much will GTA 6 cost?

$79.99 for the standard edition, $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition — confirmed by Rockstar. See our pre-order & pricing guide.
