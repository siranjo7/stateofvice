Confirmed: On October 8, 2026, Rockstar revealed the first six in-game radio stations in GTA 6, with hosts. For the first time in the series, radio and new on-demand podcasts can be played on foot through in-game earbuds and headphones connected to Jason's and Lucia's phones.

Bad Bunny is among the hosts revealed in the announcement. Rockstar called these the first six stations, so expect more at launch. GTA 5 shipped with more than a dozen.

What's new: listening on foot and podcasts

In earlier GTA games, radio only played inside vehicles. In GTA 6, Jason and Lucia can listen while walking around through earbuds or headphones paired to their phones. Rockstar is also adding on-demand podcasts made for the game's world.

Different from the album: the radio stations play licensed and in-world music. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is a separate release of 34 new original songs.

Quick FAQ

What radio stations are in GTA 6?

The first six revealed are Flash FM, Back Country Radio, Cocoteo FM, The Chamber 106.6, Afro Bank FM and Dirty South Classics. More are expected.

Can you listen to the radio on foot in GTA 6?

Yes. Jason and Lucia can listen through in-game earbuds and headphones connected to their phones.

Who hosts GTA 6 radio stations?

Hosts include Bad Bunny and Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya, among others.
