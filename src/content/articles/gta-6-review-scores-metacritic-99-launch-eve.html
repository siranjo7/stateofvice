Reported: Review scores for Grand Theft Auto VI went live on Nov 18, the day before launch. Outlets report 99 on Metacritic and 98 to 99 on OpenCritic. These are independent critic scores, not Rockstar statements.

Critics have given GTA 6 some of the highest scores on record. Roundups from Beebom, Dot Esports and others list 10/10 from IGN and GameSpot. The game launches today, Nov 19, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The scores reported so far

  • Metacritic: 99, according to most roundups.
  • OpenCritic: 98 or 99, depending on the outlet and the time of check.
  • IGN: 10/10. GameSpot: 10/10.
  • VGC, GamesRadar+, Eurogamer: 5/5. TechRadar: 9/10.

Beebom lists these outlet scores. Some roundups report different numbers for individual outlets, such as Game Informer. We list only scores that appear in the Beebom roundup.

Not final: Aggregate scores move as more reviews arrive, and review counts differ between roundups. Claims that the score ties or beats Ocarina of Time as the top-rated game come from single outlets and depend on how Metacritic ranks ties. Check Metacritic and OpenCritic for live numbers. User scores open after release.

What critics did not love

Coverage is overwhelmingly positive. Some roundups mention that the game runs at a 30 FPS target and that a few reviewers saw dips below it. Rockstar has not announced a 60 FPS mode. See our 30 FPS story for what Rockstar has confirmed.

What this does not tell you

Reviews are opinion. They say nothing about launch-day server load, patches or sales. Take-Two has said it will share more on how GTA 6 performs after launch. It has given no sales numbers yet. Read our Take-Two earnings story for the last official word on pre-orders.

Quick FAQ

What is GTA 6's Metacritic score?

Outlets reported a Metacritic score of 99 on Nov 18-19. Aggregate scores can move as more reviews are added.

Did IGN and GameSpot review GTA 6?

Roundups from Beebom and others list 10/10 for both IGN and GameSpot. We have not read the full reviews ourselves.

Are review scores official Rockstar news?

No. Scores come from independent critics and aggregators. They are reported facts, not Rockstar statements.
