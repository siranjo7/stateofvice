Is first-person mode real?

It's possible. GTA 5's console re-release added first-person mode. But leaked footage from a development build isn't confirmation: features change, get cut or exist only for testing. Rockstar didn't mention first-person in the Extended Look. Status: rumor.

Will GTA 6 really be 200GB+?

A development build isn't the retail game. Internal builds often include debug tools and uncompressed assets that never ship. Rockstar hasn't published an official download size. Community estimates range from roughly 150GB to 200GB. What's confirmed is that digital pre-loads open November 12, 2026, so you'll have a week to download.

  • Practical advice: free up as much space as you can before November 12. Around 200GB free gives you a buffer.
