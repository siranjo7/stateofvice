Confirmed: GTA 6 launches Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That's straight from Rockstar's official Newswire and was reaffirmed by Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick on the company's August 2026 earnings call. Digital pre-loads open November 12, 2026.

The short answer

After two delays — first from Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to its current date — GTA 6 arrives November 19, 2026, on current-gen consoles only. No PC version at launch. No PS4 or Xbox One version, ever.

Could it be delayed a third time?

Never say never with Rockstar, but this date is about as locked as a release date gets. Take-Two has built its entire fiscal-year 2027 guidance — a record $8 to $8.2 billion in projected net bookings — around a November launch, and Zelnick reaffirmed the November 19 date again on Take-Two's August 2026 earnings call, describing pre-orders as off to an exceptional start. Companies don't anchor billions of dollars of guidance to a date they privately doubt.

Rockstar's own explanation for the second delay was wanting extra time to deliver "the level of polish" players expect. Since then Rockstar has run its full launch campaign: a Netflix-premiered Extended Look (August 27), the soundtrack album announcement (September 17), the first in-game radio stations (October 8) and the official launch trailer (October 15). You don't release a launch trailer for a game you plan to move.

Rumor watch: earlier in 2026 some outlets floated delay speculation tied to reported unionization efforts among Rockstar developers. With the launch trailer out and pre-loads weeks away, nothing official has changed the November 19 date — treat any "GTA 6 delayed" headline as unconfirmed unless it's sourced to Rockstar or Take-Two directly.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

This is the question PC players keep asking, so let's be precise about what's known and what isn't.

  • Confirmed: GTA 6 will not be on PC on November 19, 2026. Rockstar has only announced PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions.
  • Not announced: any PC version, launch window, or system requirements. Anything you see claiming "official GTA 6 PC specs" is made up.
Rumor watch (updated October 13, 2026): GamesBeat reported, citing four industry sources familiar with Rockstar's plans, that the PC version is planned for January–March 2027. That would be far faster than usual — GTA 5 hit PC about 18 months after consoles, and Red Dead Redemption 2 about 13 months. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed any PC window, so treat Q1 2027 as a credible report, not a date.
