Confirmed: Rockstar has added a new batch of GTA 6 screenshots to its launch gallery, per Push Square and Kotaku. The game launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Two weeks before launch, Rockstar released a large set of new images. Push Square counted 37. Kotaku said "nearly 40." We could not reach rockstargames.com from our sandbox, so we rely on those outlets.

What the screenshots show

Push Square says the images show Vice City and the wider world "at its best." Many focus on Jason and Lucia. There are glimpses of other characters, nightlife and other parts of Leonida. Kotaku says the batch teases new locations, characters and chases.

Neither outlet quotes a Rockstar statement with the release. We will not describe images we have not seen ourselves beyond what they report.

Where to find them

Push Square says Rockstar published the set in its launch gallery on rockstargames.com. For earlier official art, see our guides to the confirmed characters and the map of Leonida.

Launch details so far

Pre-load starts November 10 on PS5, per our pre-load story. A PC version has not been announced by Rockstar.

Not confirmed: how each image was captured. Rockstar has not said whether every shot is real-time gameplay. Outlets also give different image counts (37, 39, "nearly 40"). Screenshots are marketing. They are not a guarantee of how the game runs.
