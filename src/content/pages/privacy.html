State of Vice / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

State of Vice ("we", "us") is an independent, fan-run GTA 6 news and guides site at stateofvice.com, with a companion YouTube channel, @StateofVice. This page explains what information we collect, and what we don't. The short version: we collect almost nothing.

What we collect on this website

  • No accounts, no forms, no newsletter. You can't sign up for anything here, so we never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • No advertising or tracking. We don't run ads, analytics, tracking pixels or social media trackers, and the site doesn't set cookies.
  • One temporary browser note. The homepage stores a single value in your browser's session storage so its intro animation plays only once per visit. It contains no personal information, never leaves your device, and is deleted when you close the tab.

Hosting and security logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. We don't use this information to identify you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Embedded YouTube videos

Official trailers on this site are embedded from Rockstar Games' YouTube channel using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube doesn't set cookies until you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites

Our articles link to other websites, such as Rockstar Games, news outlets and stores. Some links may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you. Once you leave our site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Our YouTube uploader app

We use a small private tool, "State of Vice Uploader", to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • It is used only by the owner of State of Vice, only for the @StateofVice channel. It is not offered to the public, and no visitor or viewer ever signs in to it.
  • It requests one permission, uploading videos (youtube.upload). It doesn't read channels, comments, subscriptions or any viewer's data.
  • Its sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared or sold.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • It uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you're also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The site is about a Mature-rated game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information on this site, there's usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a privacy request, contact us and we'll reply.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example if we add analytics, ads or a newsletter, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
