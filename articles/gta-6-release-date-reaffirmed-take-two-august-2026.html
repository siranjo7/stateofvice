TAKE-TWO: NOVEMBER 19 IS LOCKED

Updated October 20, 2026By the State of Vice deskSource: Take-Two Q1 FY2027 results & earnings call
Confirmed: Take-Two's first-quarter results in early August 2026 reaffirmed that GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The company also kept its full-year outlook of $8.0–$8.2 billion in net bookings, which is built around that launch.

This was the first earnings call after GTA 6 pre-orders opened on June 25. Investors wanted two things: whether the date still held, and how pre-orders were going. Take-Two answered the first clearly and was careful about the second.

What Take-Two said

  • The date: CEO Strauss Zelnick tied the company's full-year outlook directly to "the November 19th launch of Grand Theft Auto VI."
  • Pre-orders: Zelnick called them "unprecedented and astonishing." Pre-orders had only been open for about five days of the reported quarter.
  • No numbers: Take-Two didn't give a pre-order figure. Zelnick said the company doesn't know how pre-orders will translate into final sales and doesn't believe in "claiming victory before it occurs."

Why this matters

GTA 6 has been delayed twice already: from Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to November 19. Each time, the signal that mattered was Take-Two's guidance. A company doesn't repeat a multi-billion-dollar forecast tied to a specific date unless it expects to hit that date. This August reaffirmation came less than four months before launch, which makes a third delay very unlikely.

Rumor check: any "GTA 6 delayed again" headline you see now should be sourced to Rockstar or Take-Two. Nothing official has moved the date. Since this call, Rockstar has also released the launch trailer, which confirms November 19 again.

Quick FAQ

Is GTA 6 still coming out November 19, 2026?

Yes. Take-Two reaffirmed November 19, 2026 in its August 2026 earnings, and Rockstar's October launch trailer confirms the same date.

How many GTA 6 pre-orders have there been?

Take-Two hasn't released a number. CEO Strauss Zelnick only described pre-orders as unprecedented.

Could GTA 6 still be delayed?

Nothing official suggests it. Take-Two's whole fiscal-year outlook is built on the November 19 launch.
