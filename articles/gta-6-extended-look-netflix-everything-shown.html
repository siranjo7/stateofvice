GAMEPLAY REVEAL

THE EXTENDED LOOK, EXPLAINED

Updated October 20, 2026By the State of Vice deskSources: Rockstar Newswire, Netflix / Rockstar YouTube
Confirmed: Rockstar announced Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look on August 6, 2026. The 26-minute presentation premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026 at 3 p.m. ET and was uploaded to Rockstar's YouTube channel six hours later. It was the first long look at real gameplay rather than cinematic trailers.

The Extended Look showed more of how GTA 6 actually plays than anything before it. Here's what it confirmed, sorted the State of Vice way: what Rockstar showed or said, and what's still estimates from people outside Rockstar.

What it confirmed

  • You can switch between Jason and Lucia at any time.
  • The Wanted level goes up to six stars again, after GTA 5 capped it at five. Police notice your clothing, appearance and vehicle, so changing outfits or getting rid of a known car is part of losing them.
  • Cover-based shooting, plus a slow-motion targeting mode similar to Red Dead's Dead Eye.
  • You interact with NPCs and animals through a system like Red Dead Redemption 2's. That includes petting dogs, a first for the series.
  • Side activities on screen included races, basketball, kayaking, scuba diving and hunting alligators.
  • The relationship matters: Jason and Lucia's partnership drives the story.

How long is the story?

Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson said his latest playthrough took about 80 hours. That's useful, but it's one developer's run, not an official "how long to beat" number. Your time will depend on how much side content you do.

What it didn't confirm

Map size — not confirmed: Rockstar only describes the map as "very big." Comparisons like "about three times Red Dead Redemption 2" or "twice GTA 5" come from press previews, not from Rockstar. See our map guide.
Online — not announced: GTA 6 launches without an announced online mode, and Rockstar hasn't given a multiplayer date.
Frame rate & first-person — not confirmed: claims about 30fps on base PS5 and about whether first-person mode exists come from creators and leaks, not Rockstar. Our PS5 Pro guide explains what's known about performance.

Why Netflix?

Take-Two said Netflix gave the reveal an event feel and a much bigger audience than a normal trailer drop. Some fans didn't like the timed exclusivity, but the full video went up free on YouTube the same evening.

Quick FAQ

When was the GTA 6 Extended Look released?

August 27, 2026. It premiered on Netflix at 3 p.m. ET and went up on Rockstar's YouTube channel six hours later.

How long is the GTA 6 story?

Rockstar North's Rob Nelson said his latest playthrough took about 80 hours. That's a developer's estimate, not an official completion time.

Can you switch characters in GTA 6?

Yes. The Extended Look showed you can switch between Jason and Lucia at any time.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar hasn't given an official size. It calls the map very big; specific multiples come from previews and are unconfirmed.
