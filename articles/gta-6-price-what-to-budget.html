How Much Will GTA 6 Cost? Price & What to Budget

Last updated June 18, 2026 · Official pricing expected with pre-orders on June 25

You've seen the scary headlines — "GTA 6 is $80," "the collector's edition is $230." Some of that is a fair expectation; some is a leak that's already been debunked. Here's the honest version, and a clear number to budget, before pre-orders open June 25.

What's confirmed: Pre-orders open June 25, 2026. Rockstar has not announced an official price. Anyone giving you an exact figure as fact is guessing — but we do have strong signals.

The best signal we have (and it's not a leaker)

The most reliable pointer comes from the company itself: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick indicated GTA 6 will likely land in the $70–$80 range, in line with current AAA pricing. That's about as close to an official signal as you get pre-launch — the people who own Rockstar telling you the ballpark.

One number to plan around: budget $70 for the standard edition, and be ready for $80.

The "$80 leak" — and why to ignore it

Rumor — debunked: A retail listing leaked showing an $80 standard edition and spread as fact. But a well-known retail leaker pointed out those listings used placeholder codes and filler prices — the store hadn't entered the real number yet. So "$80 confirmed" isn't real. It might still end up $80 (one analyst firm predicted exactly that), but the leaked listing everyone shared was a placeholder, not Rockstar's price.

The editions (and rumored costs)

Rockstar hasn't listed editions yet, so this is rumor — but based on recent Rockstar releases, expect roughly three tiers:

EditionLikely contentsExpected price (rumor)

What to actually budget

  • If you already own a PS5 or Xbox Series console: budget $70. That's the game. You don't need a new console — and despite the rumors, you do not need a PS5 Pro to play it.
  • If you want the online-mode bonuses: a premium tier may be worth it — but wait to see what's actually inside before paying extra.
  • If you're a collector: decide your limit before June 25 — physical editions sell out in minutes and scalpers pounce.

For most people, the honest answer is: $70, standard edition, ignore the upsells until you know what's in them.

When we'll know the real price

June 25 — pre-orders open, and Rockstar is expected to reveal official pricing, editions and bonuses. You don't have long to wait, and you don't need to act on a leak before then. Get your account and payment method ready, budget $70, and let the real price come to you. Full mechanics are in our pre-order guide.
