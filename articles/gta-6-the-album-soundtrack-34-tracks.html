GTA VI: THE ALBUM

Updated October 20, 2026By the State of Vice deskSource: Rockstar Newswire, September 17, 2026
Confirmed: On September 17, 2026, Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, a collection of 34 brand-new original tracks. It releases November 19, 2026, the same day as the game.

This isn't a compilation of licensed radio songs. Rockstar commissioned new tracks for GTA 6, and six were released early when the album was announced.

The other 28 tracks haven't been revealed yet. Expect more artist reveals before launch.

Formats

  • Streaming and digital on November 19.
  • CD and vinyl at launch. Rockstar's store has three exclusives: a limited liquid-filled vinyl, a splatter vinyl and a CD jewel case.
  • Retailer vinyl colorways with their own artwork, at stores including Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Target, Barnes & Noble, Urban Outfitters, Blood Records and independent shops.
Not the same thing: the album is separate from the in-game radio stations. Rockstar has also said that Justice composed GTA 6's original score, the music under missions, and that a score album is coming. See the radio stations and the launch trailer.
