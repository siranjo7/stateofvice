GTA 6 PRICE — OFFICIAL

Updated July 11, 2026By the State of Vice deskConfirmed by Rockstar, June 25, 2026
Confirmed: Rockstar revealed official GTA 6 pricing on June 25, 2026, the day pre-orders opened. This page has the real, sourced figures — no more placeholders.

This is the honest, official breakdown of what GTA 6 actually costs — the confirmed standard price, every edition and pre-order bonus, and where to buy. No placeholder leaks, no hype headlines. Just the numbers Rockstar put on the page.

Confirmed standard price: $79.99 for the GTA 6 standard edition on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Ultimate Edition: $99.99. Pre-orders: live now via PlayStation Store, Xbox, the Rockstar Games Store, and select retailers. Launch: November 19, 2026.

The confirmed standard price

The standard edition of GTA 6 — the full base game, no extras — is officially $79.99 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Confirmed by Rockstar Games alongside the June 25, 2026 pre-order launch.

For context on how that lands versus expectations: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick had signalled a $70–$80 range in line with current AAA pricing. The official number landed at the very top of that range — a $10 jump over the traditional $70 AAA price point.

Pre-order bonuses

The Vintage Vice City Pack is included free with every digital purchase made before November 20, 2026 (physical pre-orders get a redeemable code) — see the full contents in our pre-order & editions guide. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+.

Where to pre-order

Pre-orders are live at the PlayStation Store, Xbox, the Rockstar Games Store, and select retail partners. Our full walk-through — editions compared, what to have ready, and the fine print — is in the GTA 6 pre-order & editions guide.

What this means for the leaks

Before the reveal, the loudest number online was a leaked $80 standard-edition listing. We covered it in our price & budget breakdown as unconfirmed — the listing used placeholder filler codes, so it wasn't an official price at the time. Now that Rockstar has published the real number, here's how it held up: the leak was essentially right — $79.99 is a rounding error from the $80 figure everyone was passing around. Being skeptical of an unverified listing was still the correct call; it just turned out to be a good guess.

Reminder: you do not need a new console or a PS5 Pro to play GTA 6 — a standard PS5 or Xbox Series X|S runs it.
