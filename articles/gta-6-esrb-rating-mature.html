GTA 6 IS RATED M (17+)

Updated October 20, 2026By the State of Vice deskSource: ESRB listing for Grand Theft Auto VI
Confirmed: the ESRB rates Grand Theft Auto VI M for Mature 17+ on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The ESRB content descriptors

  • Blood and Gore
  • Intense Violence
  • Mature Humor
  • Nudity
  • Strong Language
  • Strong Sexual Content
  • Use of Drugs and Alcohol

Interactive elements: In-Game Purchases.

What this means

No surprises here. Every mainline GTA since GTA 3 has been rated M. The rating is advice for parents, not a law, but most US retailers won't sell M-rated games to under-17s without an adult. In Europe, PEGI ratings for Rockstar's GTA games have been 18 in the past. We'll confirm GTA 6's PEGI rating once it's officially published.

"In-Game Purchases" — what we know: the ESRB label applies to the product as a whole. Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode for launch, and press coverage of the Extended Look reported no microtransactions in the single-player story. We'll treat the details as unconfirmed until Rockstar spells them out.

For parents

GTA 6 is an adult crime game: graphic violence, sex scenes and drug use are part of it. If you're deciding whether it's right for your household, the ESRB's own summary on esrb.org has the most detail.

Quick FAQ

What is GTA 6 rated?

M for Mature 17+ by the ESRB.

Why is GTA 6 rated M?

Blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, strong sexual content, and use of drugs and alcohol.

Does GTA 6 have in-game purchases?

The ESRB listing includes In-Game Purchases. Rockstar hasn't detailed what those are, and no online mode is announced for launch.
