GTA 6 Pre-Orders Open June 25: Date Confirmed, Editions & Price

Updated June 24, 2026Editions & pre-order bonus now confirmed — pricing expected June 25
Confirmed (June 18, 2026): Rockstar Games has announced that Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders begin Thursday, June 25, 2026 — on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, through digital storefronts and select retail partners. The official cover art was revealed alongside the announcement. Source: Rockstar Newswire.
Confirmed (June 24, 2026): Ahead of pre-orders, Rockstar revealed an official GTA VI Ultimate Edition and the Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus on the official Grand Theft Auto VI site. Full contents and official art below. Pricing was still not included — that is expected with pre-orders on June 25.

This is the big one. After months of "around the start of summer" hints, Rockstar has put a real date on the calendar: June 25. Below is exactly what's confirmed, what's still rumor, and how to be ready so you're not fighting store crashes on the day.

What's actually confirmed

  • Pre-orders open June 25, 2026. Confirmed by Rockstar Games.
  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PS4/Xbox One, and no PC version at launch.
  • Where: digital storefronts (PlayStation Store, Xbox) and select retail partners.
  • Cover art revealed — featuring Jason, Lucia and Vice City. You can grab it on our wallpapers page.
  • Release date unchanged: November 19, 2026.
  • Ultimate Edition confirmed — Rockstar's premium edition (full contents below).
  • Pre-order bonus confirmed: the Vintage Vice City Pack.

How much will GTA 6 cost?

Not yet official: Rockstar's June 25 announcement did not include pricing. The widely-cited expectation, in line with Take-Two's earlier comments and current AAA norms, is a standard edition around $70, with premium tiers potentially reaching $80–$100 — but treat any exact number as a rumor until Rockstar publishes it. Expect official pricing at or just before pre-orders go live.

The pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

Confirmed by Rockstar: every pre-order (and every digital purchase made before November 20, 2026) includes the Vintage Vice City Pack. Physical pre-orders get it via a redeemable code. It includes:
  • Vehicle & garage: the two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier and a personal Shore Court Garage near Ocean Beach.
  • Outfits & hairstyles: Jason in a vintage pastel linen suit; Lucia in a red sequin mini dress with curls.
  • Weapon pattern: an exclusive tropical palm-tree pattern nodding to original Vice City kingpin Tommy Vercetti.

Official art: Rockstar Games.

The Ultimate Edition

Confirmed by Rockstar: there is an official GTA VI Ultimate Edition — "an exclusive collection of items threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story." Here's what's inside, on top of the base game:
  • Exclusive vehicles: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy (at Paradise Garage, Watson Bay), the Shitzu Squalo boat, plus a Safehouse stash — a Dinka Enduro motorcycle & Crest Kayak, or a modded Vapid Ganado pickup.
  • Wyman Classic Car Collection: a special commission to restore abandoned classics — four cars exclusive to this edition.
  • Exclusive businesses & shops: Sara's Unisex Salon, the Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's mod shops, the Stock 305 clothing store, and the Electric Fang tattoo shop.
  • Weapons: the his-and-hers Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers (palm-tree-etched Vice City grips), plus personalized engraved variants of Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17 pistols.
  • Vice City Style: exclusive outfits, tattoos and more for Jason and Lucia.

Official art: Rockstar Games.

Still pending: the price of the Ultimate Edition and the full tier line-up (a standard edition is a given; a physical/collector's tier is widely expected but unconfirmed). Expected with pre-orders on June 25.

Which edition should you actually buy?

Until the real editions drop (likely at pre-order launch), the honest guidance mirrors GTA 5 and RDR2:

  • Most people: standard edition. Deluxe tiers have historically been cosmetic + in-game-cash, not extra missions.
  • GTA Online players: a premium tier can pay for itself if you'll play online heavily — but wait to see what the GTA Online 2 bonuses actually include before committing.
  • Collectors: physical/collector editions sell out fast and scalp hard. If a $150+ tier is real, decide before June 25, not after.

Get ready before June 25

  1. Accounts: confirm your PSN/Xbox login and payment method work now — launch-window store crashes are a tradition.
  2. Storage: file size isn't announced, but modern Rockstar games are huge — plan to clear 150GB+ or grab an SSD expansion.
  3. Console check: PS5 or Series X|S only. If you're still on last-gen, that's the thing to sort first.
  4. Bookmark this page — we update within hours when pricing and editions go live.

Quick FAQ

When exactly do GTA 6 pre-orders open?

June 25, 2026 — confirmed by Rockstar Games. A specific time of day was not given in the announcement.

How much is the standard edition?

Not officially announced. Expected around $70, but that's an expectation, not a confirmed price.

What is the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack — confirmed by Rockstar. It includes the '55 Vapid Stanier and a Shore Court Garage, retro outfits/hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a Tommy Vercetti-style weapon pattern. Included with all digital purchases made before November 20, 2026.

What is in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

Exclusive vehicles (Grotti Cheetah, Vapid Dominator Buggy, Shitzu Squalo and more), the Wyman Classic Car Collection, exclusive shops (Sara's Salon, Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie's, Stock 305, Electric Fang), the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, and Vice City Style outfits. Price expected June 25.

Is there early access for GTA 6?

Leaks claim higher-priced tiers may include early access. Not confirmed by Rockstar.

Will pre-ordering get me into a beta?

No GTA 6 beta has been announced. "Beta key" offers are scams — Rockstar has repeatedly warned about these.

Can I pre-order a PC copy?

No. There is no PC version announced. See our platforms breakdown.
