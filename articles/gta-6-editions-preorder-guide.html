GTA 6 Pre-Orders Open: $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate

Updated July 11, 2026Pricing, editions & pre-order bonuses all confirmed
Confirmed (June 18, 2026): Rockstar Games announced that Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders begin Thursday, June 25, 2026 — on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, through digital storefronts and select retail partners. The official cover art was revealed alongside the announcement. Source: Rockstar Newswire.
Confirmed (June 24, 2026): Ahead of pre-orders, Rockstar revealed an official GTA VI Ultimate Edition and the Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus on the official Grand Theft Auto VI site. Full contents and official art below.
Confirmed (June 25, 2026): Rockstar revealed official pricing with pre-orders live: Standard Edition $79.99, Ultimate Edition $99.99, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+ on top of the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Pre-orders are open now. Below is exactly what's confirmed, what's still rumor, and how to be ready so you're not fighting store crashes on launch day.

What's actually confirmed

  • Pre-orders are open as of June 25, 2026. Confirmed by Rockstar Games.
  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PS4/Xbox One, and no PC version at launch.
  • Where: digital storefronts (PlayStation Store, Xbox, Rockstar Games Store) and select retail partners.
  • Cover art revealed — featuring Jason, Lucia and Vice City. You can grab it on our wallpapers page.
  • Release date unchanged: November 19, 2026.
  • Standard Edition price confirmed: $79.99.
  • Ultimate Edition confirmed — $99.99 (full contents below).
  • Pre-order bonuses confirmed: the Vintage Vice City Pack, plus a free month of GTA+ with digital pre-orders.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

Confirmed: the GTA 6 standard edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99, both on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The official number landed at the top of the $70–$80 range Take-Two had signalled, and lines up almost exactly with the widely-shared (and previously unconfirmed) "$80 leak." See our price & budget breakdown for the full story.

The pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

Confirmed by Rockstar: every pre-order (and every digital purchase made before November 20, 2026) includes the Vintage Vice City Pack. Physical pre-orders get it via a redeemable code. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+. The Vintage Vice City Pack includes:
  • Vehicle & garage: the two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier and a personal Shore Court Garage near Ocean Beach.
  • Outfits & hairstyles: Jason in a vintage pastel linen suit; Lucia in a red sequin mini dress with curls.
  • Weapon pattern: an exclusive tropical palm-tree pattern nodding to original Vice City kingpin Tommy Vercetti.

Official art: Rockstar Games.

The Ultimate Edition — $99.99

Confirmed by Rockstar: the official GTA VI Ultimate Edition is $99.99 — "an exclusive collection of items threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story." Here's what's inside, on top of the base game:
  • Exclusive vehicles: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy (at Paradise Garage, Watson Bay), the Shitzu Squalo boat, plus a Safehouse stash — a Dinka Enduro motorcycle & Crest Kayak, or a modded Vapid Ganado pickup.
  • Wyman Classic Car Collection: a special commission to restore abandoned classics — four cars exclusive to this edition.
  • Exclusive businesses & shops: Sara's Unisex Salon, the Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's mod shops, the Stock 305 clothing store, and the Electric Fang tattoo shop.
  • Weapons: the his-and-hers Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers (palm-tree-etched Vice City grips), plus personalized engraved variants of Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17 pistols.
  • Vice City Style: exclusive outfits, tattoos and more for Jason and Lucia.

Official art: Rockstar Games.

Still unconfirmed: a physical/collector's tier has not been announced by Rockstar. Only Standard ($79.99) and Ultimate ($99.99) are confirmed as of this update.

Which edition should you actually buy?

Now that real pricing is out, the honest guidance mirrors GTA 5 and RDR2:

  • Most people: standard edition ($79.99). Deluxe tiers have historically been cosmetic + in-game-cash, not extra missions.
  • GTA Online players: the $99.99 Ultimate Edition can pay for itself if you'll play online heavily — but wait to see what the GTA Online 2 bonuses actually include before committing.
  • Collectors: no physical/collector's tier is confirmed yet. If one appears, expect it to sell out fast and get scalped hard — decide your limit in advance.

Get ready to pre-order

  1. Accounts: confirm your PSN/Xbox login and payment method work — launch-window store crashes are a tradition.
  2. Storage: file size isn't announced, but modern Rockstar games are huge — plan to clear 150GB+ or grab an SSD expansion.
  3. Console check: PS5 or Series X|S only. If you're still on last-gen, that's the thing to sort first.
  4. Bookmark this page — we update it as new details land.
