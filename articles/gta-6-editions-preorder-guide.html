GTA 6 Pre-Orders & Editions: Price, Tiers and When You Can Buy

Last updated June 12, 2026 · We'll update this page within hours of pre-orders opening

Status check (June 12, 2026): Pre-orders are not open yet. Rockstar has not announced official pricing or editions. Anyone selling you a "GTA 6 pre-order" right now is selling a placeholder or a scam.

When will pre-orders open?

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said the marketing campaign will begin "around the start of summer" — industry watchers read that as late June or early July 2026, most likely alongside Trailer 3. Rockstar historically opens pre-orders the same moment a major marketing beat lands, so that's the window to watch.

Bookmark this page — it will be updated the day it happens.

How much will GTA 6 cost?

Not officially announced. The best signal we have: Zelnick indicated in early 2026 that the standard edition is expected in the $70–$80 range, in line with current AAA pricing. Persistent "$100 base game" rumors have never been supported by Take-Two statements.

The six-edition leak

Rumor — not confirmed: A May 2026 retail leak pointed to six editions (three digital, three physical), roughly shaping up as: a standard edition (~$70–$80), a premium edition with digital bonuses (reportedly ~$99.99), and a collector's edition with physical extras ($150+). Some leaks also claim an early-access period for higher tiers. Treat all of it as plausible-but-unverified until Rockstar speaks.

Which edition should you actually buy?

Until real editions are announced, the honest guidance is the same advice that held for GTA 5 and RDR2:

  • Most people: standard edition. Rockstar's deluxe tiers have historically bundled cosmetic and in-game-cash bonuses, not story content.
  • GTA Online players: the premium tier usually pays for itself if you'll play the online mode heavily — but wait to see what GTA Online 2 bonuses actually include.
  • Collectors: collector's editions sell out instantly and scalp hard. If the leak is right about a $150+ physical tier, decide before pre-orders open, not after.

Get ready before the rush

  1. Storage: file size isn't announced, but modern Rockstar games are enormous — clear 150GB+ on your console or be ready to buy an SSD expansion.
  2. Accounts: make sure your PSN/Xbox account and payment method work now. Launch-window store crashes are a tradition.
  3. Console: playing on PS5 or Series X|S only — there's no PS4/Xbox One version and no PC version at launch.

Quick FAQ

Is there early access for GTA 6?

Leaks claim higher-priced tiers may include early access. Not confirmed by Rockstar.

Will pre-ordering get me into a beta?

No GTA 6 beta has been announced. "Beta key" offers circulating online are scams — Rockstar has repeatedly warned about these.

Can I pre-order a PC copy?

No. There is no PC version announced. See our platforms breakdown.
