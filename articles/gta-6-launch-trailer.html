THE GTA 6 LAUNCH TRAILER

Updated October 20, 2026By the State of Vice deskSource: Rockstar Newswire, October 15, 2026
Confirmed: Rockstar released the official Grand Theft Auto VI launch trailer on October 15, 2026, with no advance notice, 35 days before release. Rockstar says it was captured in-game on PlayStation 5. It confirms the November 19, 2026 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Rockstar's third major trailer, after December 2023's "Vice City" reveal and May 2025's "Jason & Lucia," is a 90-second video focused on the two leads.

What it shows

  • Jason's past: the trailer says Jason got into trouble while in the army and didn't leave on good terms.
  • Jason and Lucia as a couple: the trailer centers on their relationship and ends with the two of them jumping from a balcony together.
  • Action: heists, fistfights, gunfights, motorcycle stunts, police chases, and Lucia jumping from a speeding car onto a moving plane.

The music

The trailer debuts "Flower In A Hurricane", a new song from French duo Justice featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard. Rockstar also confirmed that Justice composed GTA 6's original score, the music that plays during missions and in the open world, and said a score album is coming. This is separate from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.

Wasn't there a "Trailer 3" in August?

The August 27 Extended Look was a 26-minute gameplay presentation, not a numbered trailer. The October 15 video is Rockstar's official launch trailer, which most fans count as the third proper trailer.

What's next: pre-loads open November 12 and the game unlocks November 19. Rockstar hasn't announced exact unlock times yet. We'll update our release date guide when it does.

Quick FAQ

When did the GTA 6 launch trailer come out?

October 15, 2026, on Rockstar's YouTube channel, X account and the official GTA VI website.

Is the GTA 6 launch trailer Trailer 3?

Effectively yes. It's Rockstar's third proper trailer after the 2023 and 2025 trailers. The August Extended Look was a longer gameplay presentation.

Who made the music in the GTA 6 launch trailer?

Justice, who Rockstar says also composed GTA 6's original score.
