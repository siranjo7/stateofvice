GTA 6 Release Date, Platforms & the PC Question — What's Actually Confirmed

Last updated July 11, 2026Sources: Rockstar Newswire, Take-Two earnings calls
Confirmed: GTA 6 launches Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That's straight from Rockstar's official Newswire and was reaffirmed by Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick on the company's May 2026 earnings call.

The short answer

After two delays — first from Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to its current date — GTA 6 arrives November 19, 2026, on current-gen consoles only. No PC version at launch. No PS4 or Xbox One version, ever.

Could it be delayed a third time?

Never say never with Rockstar, but this date is about as locked as a release date gets. Take-Two has built its entire fiscal-year 2027 guidance — a record $8 to $8.2 billion in projected net bookings — around a November launch, and Zelnick has publicly reaffirmed the date as recently as late May 2026, including telling investors the game won't be delayed again. Companies don't anchor billions of dollars of guidance to a date they privately doubt.

Rockstar's own explanation for the second delay was wanting extra time to deliver "the level of polish" players expect. Pre-orders are now open, and marketing has ramped up accordingly — another sign the date is real: you don't run a pre-order campaign for a game you plan to move.

Rumor watch: some outlets have floated delay speculation tied to reported unionization efforts among Rockstar developers during final crunch. Nothing official has changed the November 19 date — treat any "GTA 6 delayed" headline as unconfirmed unless it's sourced to Rockstar or Take-Two directly.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

This is the question PC players keep asking, so let's be precise about what's known and what isn't.

  • Confirmed: GTA 6 will not be on PC on November 19, 2026. Rockstar has only announced PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions.
  • Not announced: any PC version, launch window, or system requirements. Anything you see claiming "official GTA 6 PC specs" is made up.
Rumor watch: Reports citing former Rockstar developers point to a PC release possibly targeting early 2027 — some claim February 2027. Treat this as unverified. What we can say from history: GTA 5 hit PC about 18 months after consoles, and Red Dead Redemption 2 took about 13 months. A 2027–2028 PC release fits the pattern, but Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

Quick FAQ

What time will GTA 6 unlock?

Not announced. Rockstar typically confirms unlock times close to launch — we'll update this page when it does.

Will GTA 6 be on Game Pass?

There is no indication of that. Take-Two sells its biggest releases at full price; do not expect GTA 6 on any subscription service at launch.
