THE OCTOBER LEAKS: WHAT'S REAL

Updated October 20, 2026By the State of Vice deskSources: press coverage of the leaks; no Rockstar statement
Rumor: in October 2026, leakers posted new GTA 6 gameplay footage they say comes from a development build, including one upload of about 25 minutes. Some clips suggest a first-person mode. Separately, a leaker going by Cyberleek claims the development build is over 200GB. Rockstar hasn't commented and hasn't confirmed any of it.

Leaks a month before launch are expected. Here's how to read these without getting spoiled or misled.

Is first-person mode real?

It's possible. GTA 5's console re-release added first-person mode. But leaked footage from a development build isn't confirmation: features change, get cut or exist only for testing. Rockstar didn't mention first-person in the Extended Look. Status: rumor.

Will GTA 6 really be 200GB+?

A development build isn't the retail game. Internal builds often include debug tools and uncompressed assets that never ship. Rockstar hasn't published an official download size. Community estimates range from roughly 150GB to 200GB. What's confirmed is that digital pre-loads open November 12, 2026, so you'll have a week to download.

  • Practical advice: free up as much space as you can before November 12. Around 200GB free gives you a buffer.
  • Physical copies come as a download code in the box, not a game disc, so they need the full download too.
Spoiler warning: leaked footage may include story moments. If you want to go in blind, mute "GTA 6 leak" keywords on social media until launch.

Quick FAQ

Is GTA 6 first-person confirmed?

No. Leaked development-build footage hints at it, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed a first-person mode.

How many GB is GTA 6?

Rockstar hasn't published a file size. Leaks claim a 200GB+ development build, which isn't the same as the retail download.

When can I pre-load GTA 6?

Digital pre-loads open November 12, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
