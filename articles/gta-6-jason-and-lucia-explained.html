Who Are Jason and Lucia? GTA 6's Dual Protagonists Explained

Last updated June 12, 2026Based on Rockstar's official character pages and trailers
Confirmed: GTA 6 has two playable protagonists — Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — a couple navigating Leonida's criminal world together. Lucia is the first female lead in a modern mainline GTA.

Every GTA reinvents what a protagonist is. GTA 5 gave us three. GTA 6 does something the series has never done: two leads who aren't rivals or strangers — they're together, in every sense. Rockstar's official material leans hard into a modern Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic, and the character pages it published reveal more about the story than any trailer so far.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia grew up in Liberty City — the GTA universe's New York — where, per Rockstar's official description, her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk. Life kept swinging at her: she ended up in Leonida Penitentiary for protecting her family, the specifics of which Rockstar hasn't revealed. The story appears to open around her release — official material describes her getting out through sheer luck and wanting to play it smart from here on.

That prison opening matters for gameplay speculation: both trailers frame Lucia as the emotional engine of the story — the planner, the one with everything to lose.

Jason Duval

Jason, per Rockstar, "wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." He grew up around grifters and crooks, did an Army stint to escape his troubled teens, and washed up in the Leonida Keys doing what he knows best — working for local drug runners. He lives rent-free in a property owned by veteran smuggler Brian Heder, paying his way by helping with shakedowns.

If Lucia is the planner, Jason is the operator — the one already embedded in Leonida's smuggling economy when the story starts. (For where the Keys sit in the world, see our full map breakdown.)

The confirmed supporting cast

Rockstar's official site introduced a wider cast — three names worth knowing:

  • Cal Hampton — Jason's friend, a conspiracy-minded homebody who snoops on Coast Guard radio with a few beers. Rockstar calls him "the low tide of America."
  • Boobie Ike — a Vice City street legend who turned his past into a legitimate empire: real estate, a strip club, a recording studio. All smiles until it's time to talk business.
  • Brian Heder — a drug runner from the golden age of Keys smuggling, still moving product through his boat yard with his third wife Lori. Jason's landlord and likely first employer in the story.

How will two protagonists actually work?

Not yet confirmed: Rockstar hasn't detailed the switching mechanics. GTA 5's instant character-switching is the obvious precedent, and trailer footage shows the pair operating both together and separately — robbing a store side by side in one scene. Whether you can switch freely, or the story dictates who you play, remains officially unanswered. Treat any "confirmed switching" video as speculation.

Why this duo changes the formula

Every previous 3D-era GTA put you inside one perspective on a criminal world (or three parallel ones in GTA 5). A couple changes the stakes entirely: betrayal isn't a plot twist between factions, it's a question inside the player's own household. Rockstar's marketing keeps returning to one theme — trust — and building the entire game around a relationship is the most ambitious narrative swing the series has taken.

Quick FAQ

Can you play as both characters?

Yes — both are playable. How switching works hasn't been officially explained.

Is Lucia really the first female GTA protagonist?

First in the modern 3D era. The original top-down GTA games (1997–1999) had selectable female characters, so the precise claim is: first female lead in a mainline 3D GTA.

Who plays Jason and Lucia?

Voice and performance actors haven't been officially announced. Names floating around online are unverified — rumor until Rockstar says otherwise.
